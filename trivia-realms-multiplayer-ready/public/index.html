THE FIELD MANUAL

How to play

1 · Create or join

One player creates a private room, sends the code or invite link, and starts the match after their friend joins.

2 · Expand (first 16 turns)

On your turn, click a glowing neutral territory next to your land. Answer correctly within 20 seconds to claim it.

3 · Battle

After expansion, attack neighboring enemy territory or continue claiming neutral land. Both players answer the same question privately and at the same time. One correct answer beats an incorrect one. If both answer correctly, the faster answer wins. Exact ties or two wrong answers favor the defender.

4 · Conquer the castle

Attack the rival castle successfully twice to win instantly. Otherwise, highest score wins when the campaign ends. Each player gets one 50:50 lifeline per match.

5 · Stay connected

If a player disconnects during a question, the clock pauses until both return. The same browser can reclaim a seat automatically, unless the room is cleared by a server restart.
